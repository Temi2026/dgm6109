Three Visualization Proposals

For this project, I collected personal daily productivity data including: scheduled commitments, total time spent on planned activities, unexpected engagements, and perceived time management effectiveness (rated 1–5).

This dataset ranges approximately as follows:

  • Scheduled Commitments: 1 – 4
  • Planned Activity Time: 2.5 – 9 Hours
  • Unexpected Engagements: 0 – 6
  • Effectiveness: 1 – 5

Sketch 1 – Bubble Plot

Hypothesis:
Higher commitments improve effectiveness, but more unexpected engagements reduce it.

Data Used:

  • Scheduled Commitments (1–4)
  • Effectiveness Rating (1–5)
  • Unexpected Engagements (0–6)
  • Total Planned Time (2.5–9 hours)

Representation:

  • X-axis → Scheduled Commitments
  • Y-axis → Effectiveness
  • Bubble Size → Unexpected Engagements
  • Bubble Color → Planned Activity Time

Sketch 2 – Heatmap

As time on planned activities increases, effectiveness improves higher unexpected engagements reduce effectiveness regardless of time spent.

Representation:

  • X-axis → Planned Time
  • Y-axis → Unexpected Engagements
