How to Sign a Lease

A step-by-step guide to protect yourself when signing a rental agreement.

1. Read Before You Sign

Make sure you understand all clauses. Don't feel rushed to sign on the spot. Take a copy home to review if needed.

2. Check for Included Utilities

Confirm exactly what is included in your rent (water, internet, hydro) and what you will need to pay extra for.

3. Document Existing Damage

Before moving your belongings in, take photos and videos of any existing damage to avoid being charged for it later.

4. Know Your Rights

Familiarize yourself with the rules of the Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB) so you understand your rights as a tenant.

How to Avoid Rental Scams

Key red flags to watch out for to protect yourself and your money.

1. Never send money before viewing

Scammers often ask for a deposit or first month's rent before you've even seen the property. Always view the apartment in person or have a trusted friend view it for you.

2. Meet the landlord face-to-face

Be wary of landlords who claim they are out of the country or unable to meet you. Legitimate landlords or their licensed property managers will meet you in person to show the unit.

3. Verify property ownership

Ask for ID and ensure the person showing the unit actually has the authority to rent it out. Be highly suspicious if they cannot provide proper documentation or keys to the main entrances.

4. Trust your instincts

If a deal seems too good to be true (like a luxury apartment for half the average market price), it probably is. Don't let high-pressure tactics or a false sense of urgency force you into a quick decision.
